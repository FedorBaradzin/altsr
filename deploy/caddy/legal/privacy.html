Privacy Policy

ALTSR — altsr.space. Last updated: 11 November 2026.

This Privacy Policy explains what information ALTSR ("we", "us", the "Service") collects when you use altsr.space, how we use it, and the choices you have. ALTSR is a crypto-token screening and paper-simulation platform. We do not take custody of your funds and we do not execute real trades on your behalf.

1. Information we collect

  • Account email — if you sign in with Google, we receive your email address and basic profile (name, profile picture) solely to create and authenticate your account.
  • Wallet address — if you connect a wallet (e.g. via WalletConnect), we read your public wallet address. We never receive or store your private keys or seed phrase.
  • Usage data — basic analytics such as pages viewed and features used, to operate and improve the Service.
  • Technical data — IP address, browser type and similar data collected automatically for security and reliability.

2. How we use information

  • To provide and secure your account and the Service.
  • To display screening results, paper-trading simulations and notifications you request.
  • To improve the Service and diagnose problems.
We do not sell your personal information, and we do not use it to make automated financial decisions for you. ALTSR is informational only and is not financial advice.

3. Sharing

We share data only with service providers that help us run ALTSR — for example authentication (Google), hosting, and analytics — and only to the extent needed to operate the Service, or where required by law. These providers process data under their own terms and security obligations.

4. Data retention & security

We keep account data for as long as your account is active and delete or anonymize it on request. We use reasonable technical measures to protect data; no method of transmission or storage is 100% secure.

5. Your choices

You may request access to, correction of, or deletion of your account data, and you may disconnect a wallet or revoke Google access at any time. To make a request, contact us (section 8).

6. Children

ALTSR is not directed to anyone under 18. We do not knowingly collect data from minors.

7. Changes

We may update this policy; the "Last updated" date above reflects the latest version. Material changes will be posted on this page.

8. Contact

Questions or data requests: via our Telegram manager @altsr_manager.
